# Delete Your Vicoart Account

Vicoart users can delete their account directly from the Vicoart mobile application or request assistance with account deletion using the contact information below.

## Delete your account in the Vicoart app

To delete your account from the Vicoart mobile application:

1. Sign in to your Vicoart account.
2. Open the **Account** section.
3. Select **Delete Account**.
4. Follow the confirmation steps shown in the application.

Once account deletion is successfully confirmed, Vicoart's deletion process is designed to delete the authenticated account and remove Vicoart-managed local projects and associated locally managed project media from the device.

Deleting your Vicoart account does **not** delete original photographs stored independently in your device's photo gallery.

## If you cannot access the Vicoart app

If you no longer have access to the application or cannot use the in-app deletion function, you can request account deletion by contacting:

**privacy@vicoart.com**

Please contact us using the email address associated with your Vicoart account so that we can identify and appropriately handle your request.

For security and privacy reasons, Vicoart may need to verify that the request relates to the account you are asking us to delete.

## What is deleted

Following successful account deletion, Vicoart is designed to remove:

- your Vicoart authentication account;
- applicable authentication and session information under Vicoart's control;
- Vicoart-managed local projects on the device where the deletion process is completed;
- associated Vicoart-managed local project images, wall-selection masks, previews and project variations on that device.

Vicoart projects are currently stored primarily on the user's device and are not synchronised through a Vicoart cloud project-storage service.

## What may not be deleted by account deletion

Account deletion does not delete:

- original photographs stored independently in your device's photo gallery;
- information that Vicoart may be required to retain for legal, security or regulatory reasons, where applicable;
- information still subject to applicable temporary, backup or retention processes of Vicoart's service providers.

Temporary processing information may also be subject to the retention and deletion practices described in the Vicoart Privacy Policy.
