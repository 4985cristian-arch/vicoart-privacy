# Delete Your Vicoart Project Data

Vicoart gives users control over locally saved project data without requiring them to delete their Vicoart account.

## Delete a project in the Vicoart app

To delete a saved Vicoart project:

1. Sign in to your Vicoart account.
2. Open **My Projects**.
3. Open the project you want to remove.
4. Select the option to delete the project.
5. Confirm the deletion when prompted.

Deleting a project is designed to remove Vicoart-managed local data associated with that project from the device.

This may include:

- the local project record;
- Vicoart-managed copies of project images;
- wall-selection masks;
- generated previews;
- saved project variations.

Deleting a project does **not** delete the original photograph stored independently in your device's photo gallery.

## Keep your Vicoart account

You do not need to delete your Vicoart account in order to delete an individual saved project.

Your Vicoart account can remain active after you delete a project.

## Temporary processing data

Information previously transmitted for wall selection or preview processing may remain subject to the temporary processing and retention practices described in the Vicoart Privacy Policy.

## Account deletion
