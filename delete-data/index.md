# Delete Your Vicoart Project Data

Vicoart gives users control over locally saved project data without requiring them to delete their Vicoart account.

## Delete a project in the Vicoart app

To delete a saved Vicoart project:

1. Sign in to your Vicoart account.
2. Open **My Projects**.
3. Open the project you want to remove.
4. Select the option to delete the project.
5. Confirm the deletion when prompted.

My Projects shows projects associated with your signed-in account in the active local workspace.

Deleting a project removes that project's local record and is designed to remove its Vicoart-managed project images, wall-selection masks, generated previews and saved project variations.

Deleting a project does not delete another account's projects or original photographs stored independently in your device's photo gallery.

If media cleanup fails or is interrupted, files may remain in deletion quarantine until recovery completes.

## Keep your Vicoart account

You do not need to delete your Vicoart account in order to delete an individual saved project.

Your Vicoart account can remain active after you delete a project.

## Older or unavailable projects

Older unassigned projects are not automatically included in an account's project list.

Where explicit recovery is available, importing creates a separate account-owned copy and preserves the unassigned original. Deleting that imported copy does not delete the preserved unassigned original.

Data retained after a confirmed legacy quarantine-and-sign-out recovery action is not accessible through My Projects or legacy import in the new workspace.

The current app does not provide a restore, export or device-wide erase action for that retained data.

If the data you want removed is not accessible through the app, contact:

**privacy@vicoart.com**

## Temporary processing data

Information previously transmitted for wall selection or preview processing may remain subject to the temporary processing and retention practices described in the Vicoart Privacy Policy.
